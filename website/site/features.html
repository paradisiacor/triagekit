Safe Start for downloads, plus twelve areas from the places malware hides to why your games stutter. Each finding names the exact file, key or setting, explains why it matters, and tells you how to fix it yourself. TriageKit reads; it never changes, deletes or uploads.

Safe Start

Downloaded a game, a mod, a crack or a tool? Check it before you run it, then watch exactly what the install changes. Nothing is run, unpacked to disk or uploaded.

A file, a folder or an archive (ZIP, RAR, 7z, TAR, GZip, also nested): every program, script and shortcut inside is checked in memory.
