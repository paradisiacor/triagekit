Why people use it

Three moments TriageKit was built for.

Antivirus is good at one job: spotting known malicious files. Most real problems we see on PCs sit around that job, in settings, startup and leftovers that no antivirus reports.

The antivirus found nothing, but something's off.

Attackers rarely stop at one file. They add Defender exclusions, plant scheduled tasks and slip in certificates. TriageKit checks the places they use to stay, and tells you which ones changed.

I have no idea what this means.

Expert tools show you 900 rows and wish you luck. TriageKit ranks what matters and explains it like a good technician would: what it is, why it's a risk, where to click to fix it.

I can't install anything on this PC.

Customer machines, work laptops, a relative's PC. TriageKit runs from a USB stick, doesn't clash with the installed antivirus, and installs nothing. Reports and your licence stay on the stick, not on their PC.

Questions

Before you try it.

Short, honest answers to what people ask most.

Is TriageKit an antivirus?

No. It doesn't block, quarantine or remove anything, so it never fights with the antivirus you already have. Think of it as a check-up that tells you what to look at and how to fix it.

Will it slow my PC down?

A full check takes about 5 to 10 minutes and you can keep working. Nothing runs in the background unless you turn on daily protection, which checks once a day.

Can it catch every infection?

No tool can, and anyone who promises that is selling something. A clean result lowers the risk a lot; it can't prove a PC is free of malware. When TriageKit couldn't check something, it says so.

Why does Windows warn me before it starts?

Code signing for open-source releases is being set up with SignPath Foundation. Until a release is signed, Windows SmartScreen doesn't recognise the publisher. Compare the file's SHA-256 with the one published for each release.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

There isn't one. Every feature is free for everyone, with no time limit, ads or tracking, and the code is open so anyone can check that. TriageKit is an independent hobby project; it doesn't sell anything.
